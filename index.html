Urban Intelligence & Analytics

Understanding cities to build better futures

Cities are living, breathing systems. Every day, millions of people make choices about where to live, how to travel, where to work, and how to spend their time. These individual decisions interact with infrastructure, policies, and the environment in complex ways — often producing outcomes no one planned.

Urban Intelligence and Analytics exists to make sense of this complexity.

We use data, computer models, and artificial intelligence to understand how cities work, how they change over time, and how policy decisions can lead to better outcomes for people and places. Our aim is simple: to help decision-makers explore what might happen before choices are locked in.

What do we do?

We build realistic computer simulations of cities and their populations, allowing us to explore how urban systems interact. We are currently involved in a number of projects exploring how housing, transport, energy, health, and the environment interact. A large part of our work involves the development of agent-based models, microsimulation and synthetic population generation.

Projects

We are involved in a number of ongoing projects

AI4CI

An applied AI Hub that is developing AI for collective intelligence at a national scale.

Housing markets

Developing a UK housing ABM to explore the impacts of housing market policies.

Individual mobility

Developing mobility simulation methods accounting for activity modifications under intervention scenarios.

HealthMod

Policy modelling for health and for exploring the relationship between wealth and health.

Synthetic population generation

Using systems science approaches to understand the impacts of health interventions on populations

OPTIMA

Simulating the health impacts for populations of 15-minutes neighbourhoods.

SIPHER

Using System Science approaches to understand the impacts of health interventions on populations.

ExAMPLER

Exploring the challenges and opportunities of exascale computation for the ABM social science community

Uncertainty quantification

Understanding and visualising spatio-temporal uncertainties in urban simulations.
